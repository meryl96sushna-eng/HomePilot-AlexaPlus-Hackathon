# 3-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem
“Household planning sounds simple until one request contains deadlines, chores, study, food, and personal constraints. HomePilot is a simulated Alexa+ experience that turns that request into a transparent plan instead of giving a vague answer.”

## 0:20–1:05 — Normal plan
Enter: “Plan my evening: dinner by 7:30, laundry, 30 minutes of study, and lights out by 10:30.”
Show the generated schedule, assumptions, agent trace, and confirmation gate.

## 1:05–1:50 — Constraint handling
Enter: “I have 45 minutes before guests arrive. Prioritize the kitchen, living room, and a quick snack without overloading me.”
Point out that HomePilot detects the tight window, compresses durations, preserves buffers, and surfaces the trade-off rather than silently overpromising.

## 1:50–2:25 — Safety and explainability
Show the agent trace. Explain that external actions involving purchases, messages, accounts, or safety-sensitive devices are never represented as completed without explicit confirmation.

## 2:25–2:50 — Why Alexa+
“Alexa+ is most useful when conversation becomes orchestration. HomePilot demonstrates an interaction model where the assistant understands goals and constraints, builds an inspectable plan, and keeps the user in control.”

## 2:50–3:00 — Close
“HomePilot: from one sentence to a safe, executable household plan.”
